\begin{abstract}
\noindent This TSE analyses \textbf{Aidoc}, a clinical AI company that provides the \textbf{aiOS} --- an enterprise AI operating system for medical imaging and care coordination. Aidoc is analyzed here as a direct architectural competitor to WellnessGPT: both platforms brand themselves as ``AI Operating Systems'' for healthcare, but differ fundamentally in domain (imaging vs. workflow orchestration) and technical approach (discriminative detection vs. generative agentic AI).

\section{The aiOS --- Core Architecture}

Aidoc's \textbf{aiOS\texttrademark} is its core technical product. It serves as the infrastructure for deploying, managing, and orchestrating medical AI at scale within a health system.

\subsection{How aiOS Works}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Always-On Ingestion:} aiOS monitors all incoming DICOM images from CT/MRI scanners and cross-references incoming EHR data in real-time, without requiring a manual trigger
    \item \textbf{Autonomous Processing:} AI algorithms analyze scans in the background as soon as they arrive in the PACS
    \item \textbf{Prioritization and Alerting:} Critical findings (stroke, pulmonary embolism, hemorrhage) cause the platform to move that case to the top of the radiologist's worklist and push alerts to relevant specialist teams
    \item \textbf{Care Coordination:} Notifies multidisciplinary care teams via mobile app or desktop interface; right specialist sees right patient at the right time
    \item \textbf{Third-Party AI Integration:} aiOS can host and orchestrate third-party AI algorithms alongside Aidoc's native models (vendor-neutral platform)
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Key Architectural Principles}

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
    \item \textbf{Always-On:} No manual activation required --- AI runs continuously in the background
    \item \textbf{Alert-Fatigue Aware:} Models tuned for high specificity to avoid overwhelming clinical teams with false positives
    \item \textbf{Vendor Neutral:} Can orchestrate third-party AI models alongside native Aidoc models
    \item \textbf{Closed-Loop Notification:} Tracks whether alerts have been acknowledged and escalates if not
\end{itemize}

\section{System Flow --- Hospital Deployment}

\subsection{Onboarding}
\begin{enumerate}[noitemsep]
    \item Hospital selects clinical AI modules via Aidoc's \textbf{Solution Selection Tool}
    \item 2--3 week implementation period (zero-footprint cloud or hybrid deployment)
    \item Plugs into existing PACS (radiology viewer) and EHR (patient record system)
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Operational Workflow}
\begin{enumerate}[noitemsep]
    \item Radiologist worklist is automatically prioritized by AI urgency scores
    \item Specialist teams receive real-time push notifications for critical findings (e.g., stroke team for LVO)
    \item Alerts are tracked; unacknowledged critical alerts escalate automatically
    \item Radiology reports generated with AI-detected findings pre-highlighted
    \item Management tracks outcomes via the \textbf{Aidoc Analytics Dashboard}
\end{enumerate}

\section{Conclusion}

Aidoc's \textbf{aiOS\texttrademark} is the closest architectural parallel to WellnessGPT among competitors --- both platforms position themselves as an AI Operating System for healthcare. However, Aidoc's OS is specialized for \textbf{imaging AI orchestration}, while WellnessGPT's OS is specialized for \textbf{clinical workflow agent orchestration}. Aidoc's 17+ FDA clearances and 1,600+ hospital deployments demonstrate extraordinary regulatory and commercial traction in the imaging space. WellnessGPT's competitive advantage lies in its broader scope (triage, billing, documentation, patient management) and its unique HealthMem longitudinal memory layer, which Aidoc does not offer. The two systems are \textbf{partially complementary} at the enterprise healthcare level.
