\section{Overview}
August AI is a \textbf{consumer-facing} health assistant available on mobile and messaging channels. It emphasizes accessible symptom checking, secure storage of lab reports, and personalized tracking. It is \textbf{not} architected as a multi-agent hospital operating system; contrast with WellnessGPT's 15 specialist LangGraph agents is documented in \texttt{wellnessgpt\_architecture\_research.tex}.

\section{Reported Capabilities}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Symptom analysis and health Q\&A
    \item Long-term memory of conversations and uploaded reports
    \item Nutrition, vitals, and lifestyle tracking features
    \item Azure OpenAI-backed infrastructure (per public materials)
    \item Clear disclaimer: support tool, not a substitute for professional care
\end{itemize}
