\begin{abstract}
\noindent This TSE provides a detailed analysis of \textbf{Qure.ai}, a global leader in medical imaging AI based in Mumbai, India. Founded in 2016, Qure.ai deploys deep learning models for automated analysis of chest X-rays, head CT scans, lung nodule CT, and other imaging modalities. While WellnessGPT focuses on clinical workflow orchestration and patient management, Qure.ai focuses specifically on diagnostic imaging AI --- making it a complementary competitor in the broader healthcare AI landscape.
\end{abstract}\newpage

\section{Introduction and Overview}

\subsection{What is Qure.ai?}
\textbf{Qure.ai} is an AI-powered medical imaging company that develops deep learning algorithms to automatically detect, localize, and prioritize clinical findings in medical images. \section{Product Portfolio}

Qure.ai offers a suite of domain-specific AI products, each targeting a distinct imaging modality and clinical specialty.

\subsection{qXR --- Chest X-Ray AI}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
    \item Detects and localizes \textbf{29+ findings} on chest X-rays including TB, pneumonia, pleural effusion, pneumothorax, and COVID-19 signs
    \item Reduces TB confirmatory test turnaround time from weeks to hours
    \item \textbf{60\% cost reduction} in TB confirmatory testing in public health settings
    \item \textbf{FDA 510(k)} cleared for Pneumothorax and Pleural Effusion
    \item \textbf{WHO-evaluated} for TB screening in low-resource settings
    \item Deployed in national TB programs across India, Philippines, Nigeria, and others
\end{itemize}

\subsection{qER --- Neurocritical Care AI}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
    \item Processes non-contrast Head CT scans for life-threatening anomalies
    \item Detects: Intracranial Hemorrhage (ICH), Large Vessel Occlusion (LVO), Midline Shift, Hydrocephalus, skull fractures
    \item \textbf{100\% sensitivity} for ICH detection in clinical validation studies
    \item Reduces time to intervention by \textbf{30\%} in emergency departments
    \item \textbf{CE Class IIb} and \textbf{FDA cleared} for multiple findings
\end{itemize}

\subsection{qLung --- Lung Cancer Screening AI}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
    \item Detects, segments, and measures lung nodules on CT scans
    \item Provides malignancy risk scoring using Fleischner Society guidelines
    \item Longitudinal tracking: compares current vs. prior scans to calculate growth rate
    \item \textbf{FDA cleared and CE marked}
\end{itemize}

\section{System Flow --- Hospital Deployment}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Image Acquisition:} Patient undergoes X-ray or CT scan at the hospital
    \item \textbf{Automated Processing:} DICOM image routed to Qure AI server before reaching radiologist workstation
    \item \textbf{Triage and Alerting:} Critical findings (stroke, pneumothorax) trigger alerts to clinical team smartphones within seconds
    \item \textbf{Assisted Reporting:} Radiologist opens the study; AI-detected findings are highlighted, pre-filled draft report available
    \item \textbf{Clinical Decision:} Clinician reviews AI findings, confirms or corrects, and finalizes the diagnostic report
\end{enumerate}

\section{Conclusion}

Qure.ai is the world's most validated AI platform for \textbf{medical imaging diagnostics}. Its 1-billion-image training corpus, 100+ peer-reviewed publications, and global regulatory clearances (FDA, CE, WHO) represent an extraordinary clinical evidence moat. As an Indian company with global reach, Qure.ai occupies a distinct but adjacent market to WellnessGPT: where WellnessGPT provides the \textbf{workflow orchestration OS}, Qure.ai provides the \textbf{diagnostic intelligence layer} for imaging. A hospital deploying WellnessGPT for workflow automation could simultaneously deploy Qure.ai for radiology AI, making them more \textbf{complementary than competitive}.
